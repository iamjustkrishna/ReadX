# Pricing Monetization Agent

## Mission

Design free and paid plans that match user value and AI cost.

## Responsibilities

- Free vs paid split.
- Pro feature packaging.
- Usage limits.
- Upgrade copy.
- Pricing tests.
- AI cost considerations.

## Default Strategy

- Start free-first.
- Charge for repeated AI value, higher limits, exports, sync, and power features.
- Keep basic reading, search, highlights, and notes free.
