# Marketing Psychology

## Main Emotions

- Relief from confusion.
- Momentum while reading.
- Confidence before exams or decisions.
- Calm organization.
- Curiosity about what the document contains.

## User Beliefs To Reinforce

- "I can understand this."
- "I do not have to leave the PDF to get help."
- "My notes and highlights are useful later."
- "AI is helping me read, not replacing my judgment."

## Copy Principles

- Use concrete situations.
- Show one result at a time.
- Prefer simple words.
- Avoid sounding like a generic AI tool.
- Make the app feel practical, not magical.

## Examples

- Weak: "Revolutionize your PDFs with AI."
- Better: "Select a confusing paragraph. ReadX explains it without leaving the PDF."

- Weak: "Supercharge productivity."
- Better: "Ask your PDF what the chapter is trying to say."
