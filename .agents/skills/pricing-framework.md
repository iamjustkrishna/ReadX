# Pricing Framework

## Free Tier Goal

Free users should trust ReadX as a real PDF reader before they hit a paywall.

## Free Tier

- Read PDFs.
- Scan device PDFs.
- Open from file picker.
- Search.
- Highlights.
- Notes.
- Recent documents.
- Limited selected-text AI actions.
- Limited document chat.

## Pro Tier

- Higher AI limits.
- Full document chat.
- Longer retrieved context.
- Unlimited explain, simplify, translate within fair use.
- Smart notes export.
- Advanced reading analytics.
- Cloud backup/sync later.
- Provider/model flexibility for power users.

## Upgrade Psychology

- Trigger upgrade after the user has experienced value.
- Use limits as clear boundaries, not surprises.
- Explain what Pro unlocks in time saved and study flow improved.
- Avoid blocking core reading.

## Early Pricing Default

- Monthly plan.
- Yearly plan with discount.
- Optional early-supporter lifetime deal only during launch.

## Metrics To Watch

- PDFs opened per user.
- AI actions per user.
- Document chat questions per user.
- Highlight and notes usage.
- AI cost per active user.
- Free-to-paid conversion after first AI success.
