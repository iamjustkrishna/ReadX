# Pricing Review Workflow

## Steps

1. List current free features.
2. List current AI-cost-driving features.
3. Decide what must stay free for trust.
4. Decide what belongs in Pro.
5. Estimate rough AI usage risk.
6. Draft upgrade copy.
7. Update `state/decisions.md` if pricing policy changes.

## Default Split

- Free: reader basics plus limited AI.
- Pro: higher AI limits, full chat, exports, analytics, sync, and power features.
