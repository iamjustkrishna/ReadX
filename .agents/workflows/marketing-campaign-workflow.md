# Marketing Campaign Workflow

## Steps

1. Pick one audience: students, researchers, self-learners, or productivity readers.
2. Pick one product promise.
3. Create platform-specific posts.
4. Create visual or video briefs.
5. Add a posting calendar.
6. Update `state/launch.md` when launch assets are ready.

## Required Outputs

- X posts.
- LinkedIn posts.
- Reel or Shorts scripts.
- Screenshot or video briefs.
- CTA variants.

## Quality Bar

- Every post must map to a real ReadX feature.
- Every visual brief must show the app or a realistic app workflow.
