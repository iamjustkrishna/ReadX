# Content Repurposing Workflow

## Input

One ReadX feature, demo, or product update.

## Output

- 1 X short post.
- 1 X thread.
- 1 LinkedIn post.
- 1 Reel script.
- 1 YouTube Short script.
- 1 screenshot caption set.

## Steps

1. Identify the user pain.
2. Describe the app action.
3. Describe the user outcome.
4. Adapt tone and length per platform.
5. Add CTA and visual brief.
