# Launch State

Last updated: 2026-08-16

## Status

Pre-launch. The product appears to have core reading and AI features, but launch assets, pricing, store listing, and social calendar are not finalized.

## Launch Positioning

ReadX is for people who read PDFs on Android and want help understanding what they read.

Short tagline options:

- AI PDF reader for faster understanding.
- Read smarter PDFs on Android.
- Highlight, ask, and understand your PDFs.

## Launch Checklist

- Build passes locally.
- Core reading flow works from scanned PDFs and file picker.
- AI settings flow works for at least one provider.
- Selected-text explain/simplify/translate works.
- Document chat returns answers with page references when context exists.
- Highlights and notes survive app restart.
- App icon, splash, and screenshots are ready.
- Privacy copy explains AI text handling.
- Free vs Pro plan is clear.
- Launch posts are drafted for X, LinkedIn, Instagram/Reels, YouTube Shorts.

## First Launch Content

- 1 main demo video: open PDF, select text, explain with AI.
- 1 document chat demo: ask a question and show page citations.
- 1 highlights/notes demo.
- 1 founder post explaining why ReadX exists.
- 1 student-focused post.
- 1 researcher/self-learner post.

## Channels

- X for build-in-public and short demos.
- LinkedIn for productivity and learning framing.
- Instagram/Reels and YouTube Shorts for visual demos.
- Product Hunt when the app has a stable public download.
- Reddit only with useful context and community-specific value.
