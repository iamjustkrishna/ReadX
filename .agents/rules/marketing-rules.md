# Marketing Rules

## Positioning

- ReadX is an AI-powered PDF reader for understanding, studying, and annotating PDFs.
- Do not position it as a replacement for studying or thinking.
- Promise speed, clarity, confidence, and better recall.

## Content Principles

- Show the app doing real work.
- Lead with a concrete reading pain.
- Use short, specific claims.
- Use demos over slogans.
- Prefer before/after examples.
- Make the reader feel in control.

## Emotional Angles

- Relief: "I finally understand this paragraph."
- Momentum: "I can keep reading instead of getting stuck."
- Confidence: "I can ask questions and check the source."
- Calm productivity: "My highlights and notes stay organized."

## Avoid

- Fake virality.
- Overpromising grades, research quality, or factual perfection.
- Spammy hashtag blocks.
- Claims that AI always understands the full document.
- Posting the same exact caption on every platform.
